WordBreak

Select adjacent letters to make words — but finding a word doesn’t end it, it starts a fight over it.

1 · Claim

Trace a word through touching letters — any direction, diagonals count, no letter twice. Selecting commits to nothing; hit enter (or the claim button) to take it, and those letters lock and your selection clears. There’s no minimum length; DO is a real claim.

Two things are shown at once, and they never mean the same thing. Colour — the ring and the rising fill — is whose claim a letter is under and how close it is to paying out. The pressed disc is only what you are tracing right now, and nobody else can see it. Here you hold DO, already half way to banking, and you’re tracing DOG over the top of it: the D and O are claimed and selected at the same time.

2 · Break

To use a letter inside a claim, the word you end up with must be strictly longer than that claim. Five beats four; four doesn’t beat four. Touch one letter of a claim and the whole claim dies — the owner gets nothing.

This applies to your own claims exactly as it does to anyone else’s. To grow a claim, trace the longer word over it and claim again: DO → DOG → DOGS, each one breaking the last and restarting its timer.

3 · Bank

Survive the hold time and the claim banks: one point per letter. The letters vanish and new ones flip in.
