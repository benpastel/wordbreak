# Additions to the ENABLE base list, which has not been revised since 1999.
#
# ENABLE's archaic tail is an asset here — it is what the "most obscure" award
# feeds on — but its blind spot is everything English gained after the dial-up
# era, and a rejected SELFIE reads as a bug rather than a rule. These are the
# words a player can reasonably expect to work.
#
# Same constraints as the base list: a-z only, 15 letters or fewer, no Q (there
# is no Q tile in the bag). Inflections are spelled out rather than generated,
# because the game rewards length and a missing plural is a dead end.
#
# Deliberately absent: initialisms (YOLO, FOMO, OBVS) and abbreviations, which
# ENABLE excludes by policy; trademarks (GOOGLE, ADBLOCK); QI, which has no
# tile; and OI, which is Collins-only. Coinages that have not settled into
# dictionaries (ADORBS, DERP) are left out — they age badly.
#
# Merge into public/words.txt with `node scripts/build-words.mjs`.
