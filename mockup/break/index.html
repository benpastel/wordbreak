A claim breaking

Red holds DUG; blue's HUGS breaks it. Each card plays one picture of the break, with the sound chosen below. The breaking word takes over most of the same tiles at that same moment, so every effect plays on a short-lived copy of red's claim over the board, while the board underneath is already blue. "Slow" plays the picture at a quarter speed, to see what it is doing.
