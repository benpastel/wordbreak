Snap, crack, shatter

Blue's HUGS breaks red's DUG. The line joining red's tiles snaps; a crack runs out from a point on each tile, one tile after the next along the word; then red's colour falls away in exactly the pieces the crack made. About half a second in all. Every break draws new cracks. Pick a sound on the right; each is timed to the picture's beats: the snap, the crack, the pieces falling.
